Bus Dwell Time & Arrival Time Prototype

A simple, intuitive prototype explaining how we calculate station dwell time from Curitiba bus GPS data and use it to accurately predict next-stop arrival times.

📋 Complete 4-Step Flowchart

1

Curitiba GPS Data

Loads real bus stop sequences, coordinates, and vehicle GPS pings (e.g. Line 021 with 113 stops).

2

Match GPS to Stops

Sets a 30m platform boundary at each station to detect whether the bus is at a stop or driving on the road.

3

Calculate Dwell Time

Separates driving time from station stopping time. Average stop time found: 11.9 seconds.

4

Predict Next Arrival

Adding dwell time to the ETA formula makes arrival predictions +14.4% more accurate!
